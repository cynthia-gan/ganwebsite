Hi!

I am a clinical psychology graduate student at the University of Toronto Scarborough, where I am co-supervised by Dr. Amanda Uliaszek and Dr. Zindel Segal.

My research currently focuses on how we can best support people with borderline personality disorder and their loved ones, with a particular emphasis on community-based interventions. I hope to eventually create indicated prevention programs for adolescents at risk of developing borderline features, and I’m especially interested in how competitiveness could factor into the treatment of this population.

I love inquiries from other aspiring psychologists and potential collaborators! I hope to hear from you.
